How the numbers are worked out
  • Everything is recalculated in your browser from the raw marks in the MEQA, MEQB and MCQ_University sheets and the two mapping sheets. Formula cells, the Final sheet and the Themes sheet are not used.
  • MEQ % is Paper A plus Paper B marks over the marks available. Exam total is 60% MEQ plus 40% MCQ. Marks are compared with 50% and 40% without rounding.
  • A student with no mark for a component is left out of that component and of the exam total. A blank question on a paper the student wrote counts as zero.
  • Flags: facility under 30% is hard, over 90% is very easy, discrimination under 0.2 is low. Review item means hard and low discrimination together.
  • Cronbach’s alpha and McDonald’s omega (total, one-factor model) use the question covariance matrix for the whole cohort.
  • When it opens, this page reads a workbook named cane_results.xlsx from its own folder. If there is none, it shows the marks built in when the page was exported. “Open workbook” analyses a file from your device without uploading it.
  • Differences between universities: Welch ANOVA with Games–Howell pairs, Kruskal–Wallis, and a chi-square test on pass rates.
